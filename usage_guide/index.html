⚡ Quick Start in 3 Easy Steps

Get up and running in under a minute

1️⃣

Launch the App

Open PowerShell or Terminal and run:

py jigsaw.py

The app opens instantly with your student profile!

2️⃣

Load or Import Lessons

Click 📂 Load to pick a lesson, or click ✏️ Edit ➔ 🪄 Import Story to paste any paragraph and auto-split it in 1 click!

3️⃣

Assemble & Speak

Click or drag scrambled puzzle blocks in order. Press L to hear the teacher voice and R to record your own!

🗺️ The 5-Step Sentence Mastery Path

The research-backed progression to help kids retain sentence grammar and vocabulary

01

🌱 Phase 1: Guided Assembly (🎯 Mastery Mode)

The student reads the sentence, hovers over unfamiliar words for instant English meanings, listens to the neural voice (L), and clicks blocks in order.

02

🧩 Phase 2: Active Recall (🧩 Fill in Blanks Mode)

1–2 key grammar connectors or vocabulary words are hidden (____). The child retrieves the missing words from memory to complete the puzzle.

03

🎧 Phase 3: Auditory Training (🎧 Listening Mode)

The question text is masked! The child listens to the spoken sentence first and reconstructs it purely from what they heard.

04

🎙️ Phase 4: Speaking Practice (🎙️ Record & ▶️ Play Me)

The student clicks 🎙️ Record (R) to speak the sentence aloud. They can play back their own voice alongside the teacher's voice to refine their accent.

05

🎓 Phase 5: Long-Term Spaced Review (🧠 SM-2 Memory)

Mastered sentences are scheduled for future review at increasing intervals (1 day ➔ 3 days ➔ 7 days ➔ 16 days ➔ 35 days). If an error is made, it resets for immediate practice today!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts Reference

Control the entire app without touching the mouse!

Shortcut Key Action Description
1 – 9, 0, A – Z Select Puzzle Block Places the corresponding numbered/lettered block onto the answer board.
Enter Next Sentence Advances to the next question when the current puzzle is solved.
BackSpace Undo Removes the last placed block from your answer.
Esc Clear Board Clears all placed blocks and resets the puzzle pool.
H Give Hint Automatically selects and places the correct next block.
S Skip Skips the current question (requeues it for review later).
L Teacher Voice Plays the neural audio pronunciation for the question.
A Hear Answer Pronounces the full assembled sentence in the answer board.
R Record Voice Starts / stops recording the student's voice from the microphone.
P Play My Voice Plays back the student's recorded audio.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions and troubleshooting tips

Q: How do I create a new lesson from a textbook or story?
Click ✏️ Edit in the top toolbar, then click 🪄 Import Story / Text. Paste any story (in Hindi, Japanese, or English), choose how many words per block (e.g. 2 or 3 words), and click 🚀 Generate Lesson. All punctuation (।, ., ?, !) will automatically be split into separate puzzle sentences!
Q: Does the app work offline without internet?
Yes! All lesson files, user profiles, Spaced Repetition memory, audio effects, and dictionary definitions are cached locally on your computer. Offline sentence solving and voice recording work completely without an active internet connection.
Q: How does Spaced Repetition work for my child?
The app uses the scientific SM-2 interval algorithm. When a child solves a sentence flawlessly, it moves to ⭐ Memorized (due in 3 days) and then 🎓 Mastered (due in 7 to 35 days). If a mistake is made or a hint is used, the sentence is flagged and reviewed again today until mastered.
Q: How do I run automated tests or test the demo bot?
To run the 41 automated unit and UI tests, run:
py -m unittest discover tests -v

To watch the AI robot automatically solve sentences live on screen for demo purposes, run:
py bot.py
