🪜 The 6-Stage Pedagogical Mastery Ladder

A cognitive progression moving students from contextual recognition to unassisted recall

01

🧩 Stage 1: Fill in Blanks Pass: 0 Mistakes

1 to 2 key vocabulary words or grammatical connectors are masked with amber placeholder slots. The child learns to scan for missing keywords and drag or click them into place.

02

🎯 Stage 2: Sentence Jigsaw Pass: 0 Hints Used

The sentence is segmented into scrambled phrase blocks. The child must reconstruct the complete sentence by understanding word order, subject-verb agreement, and clause placement.

03

🎧 Stage 3: Listening Comprehension Pass: 100% Accuracy

The visual question text is completely masked. The student listens carefully to the neural pronunciation audio and builds the sentence purely by ear.

04

🎙️ Stage 4: Voice Mastery Pass: Score $\ge$ 80%

Instead of manipulating puzzle tiles, the student speaks the answer aloud into the microphone. Ollama AI evaluates pronunciation, word substitutions, and fluency.

05

⌨️ Stage 5: Progressive Typing Blanks Pass: Score $\ge$ 85%

Bridges puzzle-solving with physical typing. The child types specific missing vocabulary words into inline keyboard inputs before moving to complete transcription.

06

✍️ Stage 6: Written Typing Studio Pass: Spelling $\ge$ 90%

The ultimate mastery milestone: typing the entire sentence from memory with correct spelling, capitalization, and punctuation (including Hindi Purna Viram ।).

🤖 Local AI Voice Coach (Powered by Ollama)

Private, offline, zero-latency artificial intelligence evaluation designed for young learners

100% Private & Local

How the AI Coach Works

Sentence Jigsaw communicates directly with your local Ollama server (http://127.0.0.1:11434). Student speech and answers are processed entirely on your personal machine with zero cloud uploads, zero tracking, and zero subscriptions.

  • Compatible with Gemma 4 (12B/26B), Qwen 3.5 (9B), and Ornith (9B)
  • Instant deterministic similarity fallback if Ollama is offline
  • Word-level sequence alignment diffs (Green = Match, Red = Missing/Extra)
Warm & Constructive

Encouraging Pedagogical Prompts

The AI evaluator uses specially crafted educator prompts. Rather than just giving a blunt percentage score, it praises what the child said correctly, gently highlights different words, and explains simple grammar rules.

  • Praise for partial recall and phonetically close words
  • Identifies substituted prepositions and tenses
  • Gentle cheerfulness: "Almost there! Let's try that verb again."

💻 Cross-Platform Setup Guide

1-Click Launchers and Prerequisites for Windows, macOS, and Linux

🪟 Windows

Windows 10 & 11

Double-click the launcher script or run via terminal:

scripts\run_windows.bat

Requires Microsoft Edge WebView2 (pre-installed on Windows 10/11).

🍎 macOS

macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon)

Double-click the shell script or launch in Terminal:

./scripts/run_mac.sh

Uses native WKWebView and Apple CoreAudio afplay/afrecord.

🐧 Linux

Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora & Arch

Install WebKitGTK and launch the app:

./scripts/run_linux.sh

Ubuntu/Debian: sudo apt install python3-gi gir1.2-webkit2-4.1 alsa-utils

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about offline usage, spaced repetition, and audio

Does Sentence Jigsaw work completely offline without internet?
Yes! All sentence decks, user profiles, SM-2 repetition schedules, UI sounds, and dictionary definitions are cached locally on your device. Only Neural TTS audio fetching requires internet access on the very first playback (which is then cached permanently on disk for offline replay). Local Ollama AI evaluation is also 100% offline.
How does the SM-2 Spaced Repetition interval work?
When a student completes a sentence flawlessly without hints, the interval between reviews expands: 1 day ➔ 3 days ➔ 7 days ➔ 16 days ➔ 35 days. If an error is made, the interval resets to 0 days, ensuring the child reviews difficult sentences today until mastery is restored.
Can multiple siblings or students use the same machine?
Absolutely. Click the Profile selector in the top-left toolbar to create or switch profiles. Each student receives isolated spaced repetition memory, custom avatar choices, independent settings, and separate exam countdown goals.
How do I change the TTS speech speed or choose Hindi voices?
Open ⚙️ Settings in the top toolbar. You can adjust the speech rate (-25% Slow, Normal, +20% Fast), select specific neural voices (e.g. hi-IN-SwaraNeural for Hindi or en-IN-NeerjaNeural for English), and toggle background sound effects.
